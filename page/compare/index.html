Without and with Bindwright

Same Claude Code, same model. This is what changes at each moment of a work day.

Moment by moment

Without With Bindwright

  • A session starts

    Without You explain the project and where you stopped, again.

    With Bindwright It reads your rules and the board, then proposes a plan.

  • Claude writes code

    Without You keep asking for small functions and real tests.

    With Bindwright Your rules ask for them every session, so you don't have to.

  • A risky command

    Without A force push to main runs like any other command.

    With Bindwright The push guard stops it before it runs.

  • A pull request opens

    Without It merges whether anyone read it or not.

    With Bindwright An AI reviews it, and if the review doesn't pass, it doesn't merge.

  • The checks run

    Without You keep checking back, or Claude keeps asking and spends your plan.

    With Bindwright ci-watch watches them on your computer and wakes the session once they finish.

  • The context fills up

    Without Claude compacts it and the details get lost.

    With Bindwright compact-handoff keeps your messages word for word, plus a hand-off note.

  • Your plan limit gets close

    Without The session stops in the middle of a task.

    With Bindwright usage-guard saves the work, pauses, and picks up after the reset.

  • You commit

    Without AI co-author lines can end up in your history.

    With Bindwright A guard keeps them out, so your commits stay yours.

  • The session ends

    Without The next one starts from scratch.

    With Bindwright The board and a hand-off note say what's done and what's next.

What stays the same

It's still Claude Code. Everything Bindwright adds is plain files you can read and edit.
